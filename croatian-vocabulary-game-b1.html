What you'll practise

The exercises cover over 170 Croatian words that B1 learners need to talk about their life in more detail. Every word comes with its English meaning. Nouns also show their gender and plural, and verbs their present and past forms.

  • Time words: prekjučer (the day before yesterday), nedavno (recently), čim (as soon as), tijekom (during), rok (deadline)
  • Weather and the environment: prognoza (forecast), magla (fog), oluja (storm), sparno (muggy), okoliš (environment)
  • Work and study: životopis (CV), plaća (salary), sastanak (meeting), ispit (exam), zaposliti se (to get a job)
  • Home and services: najam (rent), račun (bill), ljekarna (pharmacy), lozinka (password), preseliti se (to move house)
  • Feelings and opinions: mišljenje (opinion), savjet (advice), zabrinut (worried), ponosan (proud), slagati se (to agree)

Seven Croatian exercises

  • Speed quiz – pick the meaning of a word, from Croatian to English and back.
  • Memory pairs – turn over cards and match each Croatian word with its meaning.
  • Fill the gap – complete everyday sentences with the missing word, then read the translation.
  • Unscramble – put the letters back in order. LJ, NJ and DŽ count as one letter, as in the Croatian alphabet.
  • Odd one out – find the word that doesn't belong and learn the group the others share.
  • Which gender? – decide if a noun is masculine, feminine or neuter.
  • Past tense – choose the right past form for on (he) or ona (she).

Frequently asked questions

Is the Croatian vocabulary game free?

Yes. The game is completely free, with no sign-up and no account. Your miles and the words you got wrong are saved in your own browser.

What level is it for?

It is made for B1 (intermediate) learners of Croatian. If you are at A2, it is a good challenge, and the A2 exercises are a gentler start; at B2, it works as a quick review.

How does the voyage from Rijeka to Dubrovnik work?

Each correct answer moves your boat 1 or 2 miles. In most exercises you get 2 miles from the third correct answer in a row; each memory pair is worth 2 miles, and each unscrambled word 2 miles, or 1 if you use a hint. A round only counts if you make no mistakes (wrong memory pairs don't count as mistakes); otherwise the boat sails back to the last port. About six perfect rounds take you all the way to Dubrovnik.

Why does the game show gender and plural?

Every Croatian noun is masculine, feminine or neuter, and adjectives and past tense forms change with it: on je išao (he went), ona je išla (she went). That is why each noun shows its gender and plural, and each verb its present and past forms.
